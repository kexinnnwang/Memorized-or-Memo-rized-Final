The products table contains the following columns:

- product_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the product;
- category (String): the category the product belongs to;
- price (Integer): the product's price in dollars;
- stock (Integer): the number of units currently in stock;
- rating (Integer): the average customer rating out of 5;
- weight (Integer): the product's weight in grams.
