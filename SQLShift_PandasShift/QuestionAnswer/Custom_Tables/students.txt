The students table contains the following columns:

- student_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the student;
- grade (Integer): the student's current grade level;
- gpa (Integer): the student's GPA multiplied by 10 (e.g., 38 represents 3.8);
- age (Integer): the student's age in years;
- attendance (Integer): the student's attendance percentage;
- credits (Integer): the number of credits completed.
