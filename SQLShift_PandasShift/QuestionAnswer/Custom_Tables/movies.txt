The movies table contains the following columns:

- movie_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the movie;
- genre (String): the genre of the movie;
- year (Integer): the release year of the movie;
- rating (Integer): the movie's rating out of 10;
- duration (Integer): the movie's runtime in minutes;
- revenue (Integer): the movie's box office revenue in millions of dollars.
