The orders table contains the following columns:

- order_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the order;
- customer_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the customer who placed the order;
- product_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the ordered product;
- quantity (Integer): the number of units ordered;
- price (Integer): the price of the order in dollars.
