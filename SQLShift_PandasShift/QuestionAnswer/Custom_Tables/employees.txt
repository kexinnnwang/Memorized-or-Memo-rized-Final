The employees table contains the following columns:

- employee_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the employee;
- department (String): the department the employee works in;
- salary (Integer): the employee's annual salary in thousands;
- age (Integer): the employee's age in years;
- years_experience (Integer): the number of years of work experience;
- projects (Integer): the number of projects the employee has completed.
