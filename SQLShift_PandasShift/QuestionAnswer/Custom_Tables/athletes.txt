The athletes table contains the following columns:

- athlete_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the athlete;
- sport (String): the sport the athlete competes in;
- age (Integer): the athlete's age in years;
- height (Integer): the athlete's height in centimeters;
- wins (Integer): the number of career wins;
- losses (Integer): the number of career losses.
