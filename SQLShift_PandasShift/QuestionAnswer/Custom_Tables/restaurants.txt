The restaurants table contains the following columns:

- restaurant_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the restaurant;
- cuisine (String): the type of cuisine served;
- rating (Integer): the restaurant's rating out of 5;
- seats (Integer): the number of seats in the restaurant;
- avg_price (Integer): the average price per meal in dollars.
