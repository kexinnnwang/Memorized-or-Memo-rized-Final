The books table contains the following columns:

- book_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the book;
- author_id (Integer): the unique identifier of the author;
- pages (Integer): the number of pages in the book;
- year (Integer): the publication year of the book;
- price (Integer): the book's price in dollars.
